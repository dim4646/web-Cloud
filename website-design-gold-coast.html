A finished website, not a set of tools

Most website builders hand you a blank template and leave the work to you. Choosing a layout, writing the copy, resizing the images, fixing the mobile view — that is your weekend gone, and the result still looks like every other template site.

We do the opposite. You tell us about your business once, and we build the site for you. You review a private preview, request any changes in plain language, and approve it when you are happy. That is the entire job on your side.

What is included

  • Custom design — built around your business, not a stock template.
  • Copywriting — we write the words based on your questionnaire answers.
  • Mobile-first build — most Gold Coast searches happen on a phone, so that is where we start.
  • Fast loading — clean, lightweight code with no page-builder bloat.
  • Contact form — enquiries land straight in your inbox.
  • Basic SEO setup — page titles, descriptions and structured data so Google understands your site.
  • Hosting included — 12 months on the Basic and Business packages.

How it works

1. Pick your package

Choose the plan that fits and pay securely online. One fixed price, no contracts, no surprise renewals. Takes about two minutes.

2. Tell us about your business

You answer a short guided questionnaire — your services, your style preferences, your contact details. Around ten minutes, and no technical knowledge is needed.

3. Review your draft

We build the first version and send you a private preview link. You can change colours, fonts and photos yourself using our editor, or simply describe what you want in plain words and we will make the change.

4. Go live

Approve it and your site goes online. Every tweak after that updates your live site automatically.

Common questions

How much does a website cost on the Gold Coast?

Our packages are fixed price: $250 for a personal portfolio site, $549 for a single-page business site and $999 for a four to five page business site. There are no contracts or ongoing fees unless you choose the optional $35 per month maintenance add-on.

How long does it take to build?

Once you have completed the questionnaire we turn around a first draft quickly, usually within a few days. The overall timeline depends mostly on how fast you review the draft and approve it.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You answer a short questionnaire in plain English and we handle the design, the code, the hosting and the domain setup.

Can I make changes after the site goes live?

Yes. You can edit colours, fonts, text and photos yourself using our editor, or describe the change you want and we will apply it. Changes publish to your live site automatically.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The site is yours. If you later decide to move it elsewhere, you can take it with you.

Do you work with businesses outside the Gold Coast?

Yes. Everything is handled remotely, so we can work with businesses anywhere in Australia. The Gold Coast is simply where we are based.

Ready to start?

Pick a package and pay securely online, or email us first if you would like to check which option suits your business. There are no meetings, no contracts and no lock-in.
