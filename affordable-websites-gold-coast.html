Affordable website questions

Why are the packages more affordable than an agency?

We use a focused process and clear package scope. That reduces meetings, administration and unnecessary back-and-forth while keeping the final website professional.

Are there ongoing costs?

Hosting, domain registration and optional maintenance can have ongoing costs. We explain these clearly before you approve the work.

Is the cheapest package right for every business?

No. A portfolio suits a simple personal presence, while most service businesses need the Business package or a custom quote.

What is excluded from the guarantee?

The domain name and other non-refundable third-party charges are excluded. See the Refunds page for the complete terms.
