Hosting that someone else worries about

Hosting is the part of a website most business owners never want to think about — until something breaks. Renewal notices, expired certificates, a plugin update that takes the site down on a Friday afternoon. We take that off your plate entirely.

Your site, your domain, your backups and your small content updates are handled by the same team that built the site. One supplier, one point of contact, one email address when something needs changing.

What is included at $35 per month

  • Hosting — fast, secure hosting with HTTPS enabled and renewed automatically.
  • Uptime monitoring — we find out your site is down before your customers do.
  • Backups — so a mistake is an inconvenience, not a disaster.
  • Up to 30 minutes of content changes each month — new prices, updated hours, a fresh photo, a staff change.
  • Domain and certificate renewals — tracked so nothing lapses.

It is a month-to-month add-on. There is no lock-in contract and you can cancel at any time.

What counts as a small change?

Updating your opening hours, swapping a photo, changing a phone number, adding a few lines to a service description, updating prices, adding a seasonal notice. If a request is larger than the monthly allowance we will tell you before doing the work, along with what it would cost — you will never receive an unexpected invoice.

Do you have to take maintenance?

No, and we would rather be straight with you about that. The Basic and Business packages include twelve months of hosting, so you are covered for the first year regardless. Maintenance makes sense if you want changes handled for you and prefer not to think about renewals. If your site is largely static and you are comfortable leaving it alone, you can skip it.

Already have a website elsewhere?

We can often take over hosting for an existing site. Email us with your current setup — who hosts it, where the domain is registered and what the site is built with — and we will tell you honestly whether moving it is worth the effort in your case.

Common questions

What does website hosting cost?

Our maintenance and hosting add-on is $35 per month with no lock-in contract. The Basic and Business website packages already include twelve months of hosting, so the add-on becomes relevant after that period or if you want content changes handled for you.

Is hosting included when you build my site?

Yes, for twelve months on the Basic ($549) and Business ($999) packages. After that you can continue with the $35 per month plan or arrange hosting elsewhere.

What happens if my site goes down?

We monitor uptime and are alerted automatically, so in most cases we are already working on it before you notice.

Can I cancel maintenance?

Yes, at any time. It is a month-to-month arrangement with no contract and no exit fee.

Can you host a website you did not build?

Often yes, depending on how the existing site is built. Send us the details and we will give you a straight answer about whether migrating makes sense.

Do you handle domain registration?

Yes. Domains are billed separately at cost, typically $20–$80 AUD per year depending on the name, and we track the renewal so it does not lapse.

Ready to start?

Pick a package and pay securely online, or email us first if you would like to check which option suits your business. There are no meetings, no contracts and no lock-in.
