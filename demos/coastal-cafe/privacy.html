Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Coastal Café ("we", "us", "our") is a fictional demonstration business built by Web & Cloud Solutions to showcase hospitality website design. This page is a sample privacy policy for illustration purposes and does not relate to a real, operating café.

Information we collect

The enquiry form on this demo site does not send data anywhere or store it on any server — submissions are handled entirely in your browser for demonstration purposes only. If this were a live business site, the equivalent form would typically collect:

  • Your name and email address, so we can reply to your enquiry.
  • An optional phone number, if you'd like a call back.
  • The content of your message, including any booking or catering details you share.

How information would be used

On a real version of this site, enquiry details would be used only to respond to your message — for example, confirming a group booking or answering a question about the menu. We would never sell or share your details with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This demo site does not set cookies or run analytics scripts. A live version of a site like this might use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are popular, without tracking individuals across other websites.

Your rights

Under Australian privacy law, individuals generally have the right to ask what personal information a business holds about them, request corrections, and ask for it to be deleted. As this is a demonstration business, no personal information is actually collected or retained.
