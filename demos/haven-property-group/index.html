Good to Know

Appraisals & inspections, answered

How long does a property appraisal take?

A typical in-home appraisal takes around 30 to 45 minutes. One of our local agents will walk through your property, discuss recent comparable sales in your suburb, and talk you through current buyer demand before providing a written price guide.

Is a Haven Property Group appraisal obligation-free?

Yes. Every appraisal is free of charge and comes with no obligation to list your property with us. It's simply an honest, local read on where your home sits in today's market.

How do private inspections work?

Alongside our scheduled open home times, buyers can request a private inspection outside those hours by contacting the listing agent directly. We'll confirm a time that suits and meet you on site.

What should I bring to an open home inspection?

Just yourself. Our agents have contract documentation and building information available on site, and can arrange a building and pest inspection on request if you're considering an offer.

Local Knowledge

Suburb guides

Five stretches of coastline we know inside out — a short read before your next inspection.

Broadbeach

Walkable, beach-close and increasingly high-rise, with Kurrawa's dining strip and the Oasis precinct on the doorstep. A strong pick for lock-up-and-leave buyers.

Burleigh Heads

Headland walks, a famous point break and a village main street that never quite lost its beach-town feel. Our home turf.

Palm Beach

Quieter, family-oriented streets between Burleigh and Currumbin, with a growing café scene and some of the coast's best-value beachside blocks.

Currumbin

Creekside living, the Alley's gentle surf and a laid-back, established feel. Popular with families upsizing from further north.

Mermaid Beach

The Gold Coast's "Millionaires' Row" — Hedges Avenue beachfront homes sit alongside a growing mix of architect-designed builds.
