Programs Built To Progress

Five ways to train at Forge — pick a lane, or move between all of them as your goals change. Every program is coached, scaled and tracked.

01

Personal Training

One-on-one coaching built around your goals, your schedule and your starting point — programmed and adjusted every session.

02

Strength

Barbell-first programming focused on squat, press, pull and hinge — for lifters who want real, measurable numbers.

03

Conditioning

High-output sessions that build engine and work capacity — sleds, intervals and mixed-modal circuits.

Small Groups

Coached sessions capped at eight athletes, so form gets corrected and effort gets noticed, every time.

05

Beginner Programs

A six-week on-ramp for anyone new to structured training — technique first, confidence built from week one.

Membership & Trial FAQ

Do you offer a free trial session?

Yes. Every new member can book one free trial session, including a fitness check-in and a look at the timetable that suits their goals, before choosing a membership.

Is there a lock-in contract or joining fee?

No lock-in contracts. Basic and Unlimited memberships run week-to-week, and there is no joining fee during the current intake.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

Memberships can be frozen for travel or injury with two days' notice, and cancelled at any time with seven days' notice through the front desk or by email.

Do I need experience to join a small-group or beginner class?

No. Beginner Programs are built for people who are new to structured training, and coaches scale every small-group session to each athlete's current level.
