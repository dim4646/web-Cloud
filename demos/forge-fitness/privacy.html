Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Forge Fitness ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information in connection with this website. Forge Fitness is a fictional gym created solely as a portfolio demonstration by Web & Cloud Solutions — no real personal information is collected, stored or processed by this page.

Information We Collect

If this were a live business, the enquiry form on this site would typically collect the name, email address, phone number and training goals a visitor chooses to submit. On this demo, the form does not send data anywhere — submissions are handled entirely in your browser and are never transmitted, stored or shared.

How Information Would Be Used

  • To respond to enquiries about memberships, personal training and class bookings.
  • To contact prospective members about a free trial session.
  • To maintain accurate records for members of the gym.

Cookies & Analytics

This demo website does not set cookies, run analytics scripts or use any third-party tracking. Images are hotlinked from Unsplash's content delivery network for illustrative purposes only.

Third Parties

No information from this demo site is shared with, or sold to, any third party, because no information is collected in the first place.

Contact

For questions about this demo, contact Web & Cloud Solutions. Within the fiction of this demo, Forge Fitness can be reached at train@forgefitness.example or (07) 5500 0187.

Changes To This Policy

As this is a static demonstration page, this policy will only change if the demo itself is updated.
