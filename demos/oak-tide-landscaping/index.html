How We Work

Four steps, one point of contact

The same designer follows your project from first conversation to handover.

We walk the site with you, talk through how you live outdoors, and get a feel for budget and timeframe.

Concept plans, materials and a fixed-price proposal, refined until the whole family is happy with it.

Our crew handles earthworks, hardscape, planting and irrigation, with regular updates as the site changes.

A final walkthrough, a maintenance guide for your specific planting, and aftercare support through year one.

About Oak & Tide

Built on the Gold Coast, for the Gold Coast climate

Oak & Tide Landscaping started in Currumbin Valley in 2013, building gardens that hold up to subtropical storms, salt air and long summers outside.

We’re a small studio-and-crew team — a design side that plans the space properly, and a construction side that builds it to last. Every project is led by the same designer from first sketch to handover, on sites from the hinterland to the beachfront.

How long does a typical project take?

Small garden refreshes can run 2–3 weeks. Full design-and-build projects with decking, pool surrounds or major planting typically take 8–14 weeks depending on scope and weather.

Is the initial quote free?

Yes — an initial site visit and ballpark quote are free. A detailed fixed-price proposal follows the design stage.

Do you work on renovations, not just new builds?

Both. About half our work each year is renovating existing gardens in stages, working around what’s already established.
