About the Studio

A quieter kind of beauty studio

Luma Beauty Studio was founded on a simple idea: beauty treatments should leave you feeling more like yourself, not less.

Tucked just back from the beach at Main Beach, our studio was built as a slow, unhurried space — soft light, natural materials, and appointments that never feel rushed. We keep our client list intentionally small so every treatment gets the attention it deserves.

Our small team trains continuously in the latest brow, lash and skin techniques, but we're just as focused on the basics: listening carefully, working gently, and never pushing a treatment you don't need.
