Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Luma Beauty Studio ("we", "us", "our") is a fictional demonstration business created to showcase web design work by Web & Cloud Solutions. This page is a sample privacy policy included for illustrative purposes only — no real personal information is collected, stored or processed by this website.

1. About this demo site

This website does not have a working backend. Any form on this site (including the booking and contact forms) is for demonstration only: submissions are validated in your browser but are never sent anywhere, stored, or shared with any third party.

2. Information a real studio might collect

If Luma Beauty Studio were a real business, information collected through booking and contact forms would typically include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Preferred appointment times and treatment selections
  • Notes you choose to share, such as skin sensitivities

3. How that information would be used

In a real deployment, this kind of information would typically be used only to schedule and confirm appointments, respond to enquiries, and maintain client treatment records for safety and continuity of care — never sold or shared with unrelated third parties without consent.

4. Cookies and analytics

This demo site does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

5. Contact

Questions about this demo can be directed to the studio's illustrative contact address: studio@lumabeauty.example.
