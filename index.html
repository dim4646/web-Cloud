FAQ

Questions we get asked

If your question is not here, send it through the form below and we will answer it.

How much does a website cost?

Our packages start at $250 for a personal portfolio site, $549 for a single-page business site and $999 for a four to five page business site. Maintenance is an optional $35 per month. These are starting points for the scope described — we confirm the exact price in writing before any work begins.

How do I get started?

Send us the enquiry form, an email or a call and tell us what your business needs. We reply with a written, fixed-price quote within one to two business days. There is no obligation and nothing is charged at that stage.

How does payment work?

We do not take payments online. Once you accept the proposal we invoice a 50% deposit to begin work. The remaining 50% is invoiced after you approve the final preview and is paid before your site goes live.

How long does it take to build?

Once the scope is agreed we usually have a first draft ready within a few days. The overall timeline depends mostly on how quickly you review the draft and approve it.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You tell us about your business in plain English and we handle the design, the writing, the code, the hosting and the domain setup.

Do you handle hosting and the domain as well?

Yes. Hosting, your domain, backups and small updates are all handled by the same team, so you do not need to deal with several suppliers.

Can I make changes after the site goes live?

Yes. Just describe the change you want and we apply it. With the optional $35 per month maintenance plan, small changes are included every month.

Do you only work with Gold Coast businesses?

The Gold Coast is where we are based, and we work with businesses across Brisbane and the rest of Queensland. Everything is handled remotely, so we can work with you anywhere in Australia.
